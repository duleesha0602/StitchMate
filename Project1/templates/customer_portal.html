How to measure yourself accurately for bespoke tailoring

Atelier Guide
1. Shoulder Width
From shoulder seam to shoulder seam across upper back.
2. Bust / Chest
Around fullest part of chest, keeping tape horizontal.
3. Natural Waist
Around narrowest natural waistline above navel.
4. Hips
Around fullest part of hips & buttocks standing straight.
5. Arm & Sleeve Length
From shoulder tip down slightly bent arm to wrist bone.
6. Trouser Length & Inseam
Outseam from waist to ankle; Inseam from crotch to floor.
